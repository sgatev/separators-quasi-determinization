\section{Separators in finite-state transducers}
\label{sec_separators_finite_state_transducers}

We extend the results of the previous section by introducing separated
finite-state transducers. We study the special case when the relation represented by
such devices is a function of bounded variation and describe an efficient
deterministic representation for it.
