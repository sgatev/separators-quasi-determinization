\chapter{Separators in finite-state transducers}
\label{chapter:separators_finite_state_transducers}

In this chapter, we introduce the notion of separated finite-state transducers. We
study the special case when the relation represented by such devices is a function
of bounded variation and describe an efficient deterministic representation for it.
