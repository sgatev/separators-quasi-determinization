\chapter{Separators in finite-state automata}
\label{chapter:separators_finite_state_automata}

In this chapter, we present the notion of separated finite-state automata. We observe
some of the key properties and use them to derive an efficient deterministic
representation of such devices. We then give an example of applying these results to separated finite-state automata constructed from regular expressions.
