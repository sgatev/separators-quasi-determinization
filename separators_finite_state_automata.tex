\section{Separators in finite-state automata}
\label{sec_separators_finite_state_automata}

In this section, we define the notion of separated finite-state automata. We observe
some of the key properties and use them to derive a more efficient algorithm for
determinization of such devices. We then give an example of applying the new
construction to separated finite-state automata derived from regular expressions.
