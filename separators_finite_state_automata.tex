\chapter{Separators in finite-state automata}
\label{chapter:separators_finite_state_automata}

In this chapter, we introduce the notion of separated finite-state automata. We
observe some of the key properties and use them to derive an efficient deterministic
representation of such devices. We then give an example of applying this result to
separated finite-state automata constructed from regular expressions.
