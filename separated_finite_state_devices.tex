\section{Separators in finite-state devices}

In this section we define the notion of separated finite-state device based on
properties of the transition relation. We show that the states of such device could
be partitioned in disjoint components and that a procedure similar to determinization
can be applied to these components so that we end up with a finite-state device
that behaves deterministically.
